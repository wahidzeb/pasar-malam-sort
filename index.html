About Pasar Malam Sort

Pasar Malam Sort is a calm sorting puzzle set in a cosy Malaysian night market. Every stall is full of mixed-up snacks like satay, kuih lapis, onde-onde and ais kacang.

Tap a snack to pick it up, then tap another stall with an empty space to put it there. Line up three of the same snack on one stall and they are sold. When the front of a stall is empty, the snacks at the back move forward. Sell every snack to clear the market. There is no timer, Undo is free and a Hint is always there. Play 60 levels in the Pasar Malam, the Lantern Walk and the Ramadan Bazaar, and fill your snack book.

3 quick tips

  1. Look at the darker snacks at the back of each stall: they come forward when the front is empty.
  2. Keep one stall with free spaces so you always have room to move.
  3. A food cover shows a number. Sell that many sets of three and the cover lifts.

How to play

👆 Tap a snack to pick it up. Then tap a stall with an empty space to put it there. You can also drag it.

✨ Put three of the same snack side by side on one stall and they are sold!

📦 When the front of a stall is empty, the snacks waiting at the back move to the front.

🏮 Sell every snack to clear the market and win the level.

🧺 Some snacks hide under a food cover. The number shows how many more sets of three you need to sell before it opens.

↶ Undo is free, as many times as you like. 💡 Hint shows a good move.

⏱ No rush! There is no timer, unless you switch on Hard mode in Settings.
