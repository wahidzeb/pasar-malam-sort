# Pasar Malam Sort by Zaib

Pasar Malam Sort is a calm sorting puzzle at a cosy Malaysian night market. Move satay, kuih, ais kacang and more between the stalls, put three of a kind together to sell them and clear every stall. 60 levels in the Pasar Malam, Lantern Walk and Ramadan Bazaar, with free undo and hints. Optional Hard mode adds a timer.

Free to play, no ads during play and nothing to buy. Progress is saved on your device.
